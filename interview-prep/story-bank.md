This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### [Strategy / Risk] Global SC Strategy Under Tariff Pressure
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** US tariff escalation threatened Mercedes Vans' US supply chain with EUR 35M+ exposure.
**T (Task):** Design and execute a tariff mitigation strategy in 6 weeks.
**A (Action):** Analyzed trade flows, identified rerouting options, negotiated with customs authorities, built a 3-scenario model for CFO sign-off.
**R (Result):** EUR 8M avoided short-term; EUR 27M long-term mitigation locked.
**Reflection:** I'd build the scenario model earlier and not wait for escalation. The Digital Twin now runs scenarios continuously so we're never reactive.
**Best for questions about:** strategic planning under pressure, risk management, financial impact, executive stakeholder management

### [Cost Savings] EUR 16M Savings via Lean + Process Mining
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** Post-COVID cost pressure required structured savings across a EUR 150M supply chain budget.
**T (Task):** Design and lead a multi-lever cost reduction programme.
**A (Action):** Applied Lean, Six Sigma, Celonis process mining, and commercial contract renegotiation simultaneously across supplier, logistics, and internal cost pools.

### [AI / Digital Transformation] Building the AI Control Tower
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** Real-time supply chain decisions were still human-dependent -- no autonomous response to disruptions in a 9-country network.
**T (Task):** Design and build an AI agent framework for autonomous supply chain decisions.

### [People / Culture] Leading a 25-Person Global Team Across 9 Countries
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** Cultural misalignment across 9-country team was slowing decision-making and alignment.
**T (Task):** Build a high-performing, aligned global SC team from a dispersed, culturally diverse group.
**A (Action):** Implemented structured weekly cadence (async + sync), clear RACI framework, Hoshin Kanri for goal alignment and visual management of team targets.
**R (Result):** Team cohesion improved measurably; delivery on EUR 150M budget on track; AI projects staffed from within existing team.

**Best for questions about:** people leadership, cross-cultural management, organizational alignment, remote/distributed teams

### [Process Compliance] Multi-Country Lean Standards Enforcement — Daimler Asia
**Source:** Report #007 — Henkel — Supply Chain Innovation Category Head
**S (Situation):** Daimler Trucks Asia had inconsistent MPS compliance across Japan, India, and DTNA — audits showed 40%+ deviation in some markets; central lean function had no direct authority.
**T (Task):** Raise compliance rates across 3 markets without authority to mandate changes at country level.
**A (Action):** Built country-specific compliance dashboards (self-service); ran monthly remote calibration sessions; created escalation path to regional CEOs for repeat non-compliance.
**R (Result):** Compliance rate moved from ~60% to 92% across 3 markets within 18 months; $4M/year in Japan warranty savings attributable to standardisation.
**Reflection:** Central governance without authority only works when you make compliance easy and visible. Switch from audit reports to self-service dashboards — teams can see their own gaps before you do.
**Best for questions about:** process adherence, multi-country governance, influencing without authority, standards enforcement, Lean deployment

### [Regulatory Compliance] EUDR Supplier Compliance Framework
**Source:** Report #006 — Fresenius Medical Care — Head of Supply Chain
**S (Situation):** EUDR regulation required full material traceability across a 9-country supplier base — no internal framework existed.
**T (Task):** Build supplier due diligence and traceability system before enforcement date.
**A (Action):** Mapped all EUDR-relevant components and supply tiers; built supplier questionnaire and audit protocol; integrated traceability data into SAP S/4HANA procurement systems; trained procurement teams across all countries.
**R (Result):** Full supplier traceability achieved; zero compliance gaps at audit.
**Reflection:** Regulatory SC compliance is an ongoing operating model change, not a one-time project. This discipline — supplier mapping, audit frameworks, data traceability — maps directly to ISO 13485 supplier management in medtech. Use this story to bridge automotive credibility to regulated industries.
**Best for questions about:** regulated supply chains, supplier compliance, medtech transition, risk management, EU regulations, traceability

### [Quality / Regulated Manufacturing] AI Computer Vision Quality Gate
**Source:** Report #006 — Fresenius Medical Care — Head of Supply Chain
**S (Situation):** Mercedes-Benz Charleston Sprinter/Metris production had persistent first-time quality (FTQ) gaps driving rework cost.
**T (Task):** Deploy an automated quality inspection system at the production gate.
**A (Action):** Scoped and deployed ML-based computer vision defect detection; integrated with production SCADA; defined acceptance/rejection criteria with the quality team; trained operators to act on AI outputs.
**R (Result):** 8% improvement in FTQ; rework costs materially reduced; quality-by-design principles embedded in production line.
**Reflection:** Quality automation requires the same change management as process improvement. Getting operators to trust AI decisions is harder than building the system. Critical lesson for any regulated manufacturing environment where human-AI trust is essential.
**Best for questions about:** quality management, AI in manufacturing, regulated environments, technology adoption, quality-by-design
