# Story Bank — Master STAR+R Stories

This file accumulates your best interview stories over time. Each evaluation (Block F) adds new stories here. Instead of memorizing 100 answers, maintain 5-10 deep stories that you can bend to answer almost any behavioral question.

## How it works

1. Every time `/career-ops oferta` generates Block F (Interview Plan), new STAR+R stories get appended here
2. Before your next interview, review this file — your stories are already organized by theme
3. The "Big Three" questions can be answered with stories from this bank:
   - "Tell me about yourself" → combine 2-3 stories into a narrative
   - "Tell me about your most impactful project" → pick your highest-impact story
   - "Tell me about a conflict you resolved" → find a story with a Reflection

### [Strategy / Risk] Global SC Strategy Under Tariff Pressure
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** US tariff escalation threatened Mercedes Vans' US supply chain with EUR 35M+ exposure.
**T (Task):** Design and execute a tariff mitigation strategy in 6 weeks.
**A (Action):** Analyzed trade flows, identified rerouting options, negotiated with customs authorities, built a 3-scenario model for CFO sign-off.
**R (Result):** EUR 8M avoided short-term; EUR 27M long-term mitigation locked.
**Reflection:** I'd build the scenario model earlier and not wait for escalation. The Digital Twin now runs scenarios continuously so we're never reactive.
**Best for questions about:** strategic planning under pressure, risk management, financial impact, executive stakeholder management

### [Cost Savings] EUR 16M Savings via Lean + Process Mining
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** Post-COVID cost pressure required structured savings across a EUR 150M supply chain budget.
**T (Task):** Design and lead a multi-lever cost reduction programme.
**A (Action):** Applied Lean, Six Sigma, Celonis process mining, and commercial contract renegotiation simultaneously across supplier, logistics, and internal cost pools.
**R (Result):** EUR 16M delivered since 2024.
**Reflection:** Process mining finds quick wins (waste elimination) while contract levers take 6+ months. Sequencing and timing the two tracks matters. Launch contract renegotiations at month 1, not month 6.
**Best for questions about:** cost reduction, operational excellence, leadership of complex programs, results orientation

### [AI / Digital Transformation] Building the AI Control Tower
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** Real-time supply chain decisions were still human-dependent -- no autonomous response to disruptions in a 9-country network.
**T (Task):** Design and build an AI agent framework for autonomous supply chain decisions.
**A (Action):** Built MCP + A2A multi-agent orchestration for material planning, contract management, and financial review; integrated Digital Twin for simulation.
**R (Result):** Framework live in production, reducing reaction time on supply disruptions; planning deviations reduced.
**Reflection:** Start with one decision domain (reorder triggers) before expanding to full autonomy. We tried to boil the ocean initially and slowed delivery. Narrow scope, prove value, then expand.
**Best for questions about:** digital transformation, AI in operations, innovation, change management, technology leadership

### [People / Culture] Leading a 25-Person Global Team Across 9 Countries
**Source:** Report #005 — Beiersdorf — Supply Chain Director
**S (Situation):** Cultural misalignment across 9-country team was slowing decision-making and alignment.
**T (Task):** Build a high-performing, aligned global SC team from a dispersed, culturally diverse group.
**A (Action):** Implemented structured weekly cadence (async + sync), clear RACI framework, Hoshin Kanri for goal alignment and visual management of team targets.
**R (Result):** Team cohesion improved measurably; delivery on EUR 150M budget on track; AI projects staffed from within existing team.
**Reflection:** Hire for cultural adaptability, not just functional expertise. Skill gaps are learnable; culture gaps aren't. I now screen explicitly for cross-cultural communication style in all senior hires.
**Best for questions about:** people leadership, cross-cultural management, organizational alignment, remote/distributed teams
